# FABRIPASS brand guidelines
Demo project / Version 1 / Approved September 28, 2026

Brand promise: every product has a story. Make it a story worth knowing.
Palette: Forest #284B3A, Paper #F2F3EB, Moss #829679, Ink #253E32.
Typography: Inter for product interfaces; editorial serif accents for the brand story.
Logo clear space: at least the height of the letter f around the wordmark.
Voice: clear, specific, and optimistic. Explain the provenance behind the promise.
Accessibility: use high contrast for body copy and visible keyboard focus.
These demo assets are illustrative and can be replaced with approved client files.
